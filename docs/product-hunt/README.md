## Description (max 260 characters)
SayPay lets tradespeople send a quote as one link with a voice note explaining the job. Clients listen, accept in one tap and pay a deposit or in full — by card (straight to your bank via Stripe) or bank transfer. No app or login for clients.

## Maker's first comment
Hi Product Hunt 👋

I built SayPay because quoting is where tradespeople lose jobs. A PDF with a list of prices doesn't explain *why* a job costs what it does — and the client goes with whoever's cheapest.

SayPay fixes that:

🔊 **Add a voice note** — your client hears you explain the job in your own words. Quotes with a voice feel personal and get accepted more.
🔗 **Send one link** — by WhatsApp, text or email. No app or login for your client.
💳 **Get paid your way** — choose a deposit or full payment on every quote. Clients pay by card (Stripe, straight to your bank) or bank transfer.

It's free while we launch. I'd love your feedback — especially from anyone in the trades. What would make you switch from your current quoting setup?
